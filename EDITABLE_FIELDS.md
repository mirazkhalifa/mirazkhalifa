# Editable Fields

The public website keeps the existing design and now exposes editing through the Edit Website panel.

Editable:
- Name, position and hero text
- Hero/About images
- About text and stats
- Work Experience: add/edit/delete
- Products & Solutions heading
- Products intro text
- Every product name and description: add/edit/delete
- Let's Connect heading
- Contact title
- Contact role/company text
- Phone
- Email
- Address
- Blogs: write/edit/delete

Click **Edit Website** from the top, or click the **Edit** button beside Products & Solutions / Let's Connect.

This version stores edits in browser localStorage. For multi-device live editing, connect the same fields to Supabase.
